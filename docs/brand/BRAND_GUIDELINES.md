# PRIVIO SENTRY — Brand Guidelines v0.2

## 1. Brand core

### Name
**PRIVIO SENTRY**

### Meaning
The name combines a privacy-oriented coined term with the concept of a sentry: a guard placed at a boundary.

The brand idea is:

> **A guard between sensitive data and exposure.**

### Descriptor
**Local AI Privacy Infrastructure**

### Product descriptor
**AI-assisted sensitive-data protection**

### Preferred headline
**Protect data before it is exposed.**

### Secondary line
**Detect. Review. Protect.**

---

## 2. Logo direction

The selected visual base is the **“Balloon + Document”** concept.

The icon combines:
- rounded protected container;
- document/content symbol;
- speech/document cue;
- lower pointer suggesting a controlled output boundary.

It intentionally avoids the generic “padlock” symbol.

### Visual meaning

**Document**
→ information / source material

**Rounded enclosure**
→ protection boundary

**Three horizontal lines**
→ structured text / information

**Lower point**
→ controlled flow/output

## 4. Clear space

Use an exclusion zone equal to the height of the icon's internal text line around the logo.

Never let:
- UI elements;
- photographs;
- borders;
- other logos;
- document content

touch the mark.

---

## 5. Minimum size

For digital interfaces:

- full lockup: 120 px recommended minimum width;
- icon only: 24 px minimum;
- favicon: simplified icon only.

At very small sizes remove secondary details.

---

## 6. Color

Primary visual direction:

- Ink `#0B1220`
- Primary Blue `#2563EB`
- Privacy Teal `#14B8A6`
- Purple `#6941C6`
- Slate `#667085`
- Surface `#F8FAFC`

The logo may use the blue/purple gradient shown in the selected concept.

Monochrome versions must remain available for:
- legal documents;
- print;
- dark backgrounds;
- embossing;
- accessibility;
- system menus.

---

## 8. Tagline system

Preferred:
> Protect data before it is exposed.

Alternative:
> Detect. Review. Protect.

Product:
> Local AI for sensitive documents.

Platform:
> The privacy layer between data and AI.

Do not force a tagline under every logo occurrence.

---

## 9. Tone of voice

The brand should sound:
- precise;
- calm;
- technical;
- transparent;
- controlled.

Avoid:
- “magic”;
- exaggerated security claims;
- fear-based copy;
- “100% secure”;
- “guaranteed LGPD compliance”.

---

## 10. Logo misuse

Do not:
- rotate the mark;
- stretch it;
- recolor arbitrarily;
- add drop shadows;
- add a lock icon;
- place the logo inside a competing shield;
- place the wordmark over busy content;
- redraw the icon ad hoc.

---

## 11. Asset status

`assets/logo-reference-light.png` and `logo-reference-dark.png` are crops from the user-selected concept board.

`assets/concept-mark.svg` is a **clean geometric concept redraw**, not the final production artwork.

The final logo should be redrawn as vector artwork in the design tool before release.
